DraftReal numbers, Jul to 20 Oct 2026. For Level 3 only. This page changes nothing: the targets in use are in the ledgers.

One month, person by person. Each person has three plans: low (the leave we have now), high (nobody on leave) and the target, the middle of the two. The target splits into service, treatment and retail, and the clients to match, held to what her hours can fit. Then it adds up to the branch and the group.

Read this first. Thirty seconds.
Why this
A target is not a guess. It is her normal day, adjusted for the kind of month that is coming.
Why now
The November plans go out with the end of October emails. Mette checks them first, so this is the place to try things and adjust.
Three numbers
High is what we could sell if nobody took leave. Low is what we sell with the leave we have. The target sits in the middle, so it is a goal that still looks real. The gap between low and high is what missing staff cost.
If we do nothing
Targets stay as guesses, and the business can be planned below break-even, which is AED 1.7 to 1.8 million a month.

Where these numbers come from

Sales: Phorest staff daily (Supabase phorest_staff_daily), services plus courses ex VAT (treatments are inside services, retail is out), days with sales, 1 Jul to 20 Oct 2026. Hours: the utilisation roster (staff_utilisation), Aug and Sep: hours a day, and booked hours divided by visits for the hours a client takes. Treatment now: the ledger (branch_staff_daily), hair only. October as it was loaded on 6 Oct: staff_targets, the 80% figure (kept in a backup). Season factors: financial_totals and phorest_staff_daily, 2022 to March 2026, middle reading of each calendar month. Names are matched to Phorest by first name inside each branch and checked against the roster. Re-pull before the 25th run.

Not in the list on purpose: assistants, receptionists, coordinators and leavers who are archived. Where no October target was loaded, Coach Emma confirms the person first.
