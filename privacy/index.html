Privacy

Tara Rose Salons team site · trk-salon-os.com · updated 1 October 2026

trk-salon-os.com is the private site for the Tara Rose Salons team in the UAE and Bahrain. It holds our operating procedures, training material and, for managers, the business dashboard. It is not open to the public, and nothing on it is for sale.

Who can sign in

Only people we have added to our team list. If your email isn’t on the list, the site tells you so and shows nothing else.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Your email address and name. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares these with us; we ask for nothing else from your Google account (no contacts, no files, no calendar).
  • A password, if you choose email and password instead. It is stored only in encrypted form by our sign-in provider; nobody at Tara Rose Salons can see it.
  • When you last signed in.

How we use it

Only to check that you are on the team list and to show you the parts of the site your role allows. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell or share it, and the site carries no advertising or third-party tracking.

Where it is kept

Sign-in and the site’s data are handled by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The pages themselves are served by GitHub Pages. Both process data only to run the site for us.

Removing your account

When you leave the team, your access ends. To have your sign-in deleted at any time, ask your manager or email info@tararosesalon.com.
