The Blonde Standard

Your blonde, kept the way it left the chair.

Blonde can go warm and flat within weeks of leaving the chair. The Blonde Standard is three visits planned together: one to create your blonde, two to keep it bright. It starts with a free Confidence Mapping.

How it works

How the three visits work.

Sun, pool water, hard water and humidity pull the cool tone out of blonde. The plan tones it back as it starts to shift, and you leave your first visit with the next two booked.

Visit 1 · CreateThe blonde itself

Your colour, with bond protection at every stage.

Visit 2 · ProtectWeek 4 to 6

Hydration, and the toner refreshed before it shifts warm.

Visit 3 · Keep on trackWeek 8 to 12

Tone and shine refreshed, protection topped up, next cycle booked.

Questions

Quick answers.

How do I know which plan is mine?
Soft Blonde is brightness with lower upkeep. Signature Blonde is for women who are already blonde and want to stay that way. Transformation Blonde is for a major change, high lift or correction. You do not need to know. We work it out together at your Confidence Mapping. Balayage sits inside whichever plan you are on.
How often will I need to come in?
Visit 2 sits at week four to six, and visit 3 at week eight to twelve. Your stylist names your exact weeks in the chair, and you leave with the next two visits booked.
Will lightening damage my hair?
Lightening does put stress on hair, so protection is built in from the start. Bond protection is part of every blonde plan, before, during and after your colour, and your plan keeps it topped up between visits.
What does it cost?
Plans start from AED 1,550 for short or fine hair, and every service price is confirmed at your free Confidence Mapping, before you commit to anything.
