Plan terms

The small print, in plain English.

Read the plan terms
  1. What the plan is. Three visits, Create, Protect and Keep on Track, booked and paid together. Your plan price depends on your length and starting colour, and it is confirmed at your free Confidence Mapping before you pay anything.
  2. When the visits happen. Visit 2 is four to six weeks after Visit 1, and Visit 3 is eight to twelve weeks after it. You leave Visit 1 with the next two booked.
  3. Which plan. If your Confidence Mapping shows that another lane suits your hair better, we tell you and the plan price moves to that lane before you pay.
  4. Paying. Our team sends you a secure payment link during salon hours, 9am to 8pm. New clients secure the first appointment with a deposit, and we explain how deposits work when we reply.
  5. Changing a visit. Please give us 48 hours’ notice to move or cancel a visit. A deposit is non-refundable if the appointment is missed or cancelled late.
  6. The Confidence Promise. If something does not feel right within seven days of any colour or treatment service, we invite you back to refine it. No blame, just care.
  7. Prices. Prices shown are for a Stylist, in AED, with VAT. Your stylist level and your length are confirmed at the Confidence Mapping, before anything is booked.
  8. Your details. We use what you send only to arrange your appointments. See our Privacy Policy.
Questions

Quick answers.

How do I know which lane I am?
Soft Blonde is brightness with lower upkeep. Signature Blonde is for women who identify as blonde and want a regular rhythm. Transformation Blonde is for a major change, high lift or correction. You do not have to be right: the Confidence Mapping names it with you. Balayage sits inside whichever lane you are in.
How often will I need to come in?
Visit 2 sits at week four to six, and visit 3 at week eight to twelve. Your stylist names your exact weeks in the chair, and you leave with the next two visits booked.
Will lightening damage my hair?
Lightening always puts hair under stress, so protection has to be built in from the start. Bond protection runs through every blonde service here, before, during and after your colour, and your plan keeps it cared for between visits. That is how blonde stays healthy.
What does it cost?
Plans start from AED 1,550 for short or fine hair, and every service price is confirmed at your free Confidence Mapping, before you commit to anything.
