It is not you

Your blonde fades faster here.

You wonder why your hair is fading faster than you think it should. It’s not you; you’re just missing a plan that protects you from the local elements that affect your blonde. Brassy is the toner wearing off. In the UAE, that happens in weeks, not months.

Sun

UV opens the hair and lifts the toner out, so the warm base underneath shows through.

Pool

Chlorine and copper settle in lightened hair. That is the greenish grey cast after a weekend swim.

Hard water

Tap water here is chlorinated and stored in tanks. The residue builds up on lightened hair and dulls it.

Air conditioning

Dry indoor air all year pulls moisture out. Dry hair holds tone badly and snaps more easily at the ends.

Avoid the big fix

Refresh it before it goes wrong, not after.

Most blondes wait until it has faded, then start over, and by then the fix is expensive. The plan refreshes your blonde before it drifts, so it stays healthier week after week.

Questions

Quick answers.

How do I know which plan is mine?
You do not have to know. Soft Blonde is brightness with lower upkeep, Signature Blonde is blonde that stays right and Transformation Blonde is a big change or a correction. Message your salon on WhatsApp and a colourist works it out with you.
How often will I need to come in?
Visit 2 sits at week four to six, and visit 3 at week eight to twelve. Your stylist names your exact weeks in the chair, and you leave with the next two visits booked.
Will lightening damage my hair?
Lightening does put stress on hair, so protection is built in from the start. Bond protection is part of every blonde plan, before, during and after your colour, and your plan keeps it topped up between visits.
What does it cost?
Plans start from AED 1,550 for all three visits, VAT included. Your price depends on your plan, your hair length and thickness, and your stylist level. You see a guide price when you book, and your salon confirms it after checking your hair.
How do deposits and the Confidence Promise work?
A first highlights, balayage or colour change, or any appointment over two hours, may need a deposit to hold your time. It is never added on top: it comes off your price on the day. You can move your appointment up to two times with at least 48 hours’ notice and the deposit moves with you. With less notice it is not refundable, and same-day bookings never need one. The full details are in our deposit policy. Every colour and treatment service is covered by the Confidence Promise, set out just above the booking section.
