  1. Where it applies.

    The Blonde Standard is available at all four Tara Rose salons: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.

  2. How prices work.

    The whole plan, for all three visits, by hair length:

    PlanShort / FineMediumLong / ThickExtra Long
    Soft Blonde1,5501,7051,9602,565
    Signature Blonde1,6951,8702,1052,705
    Transformation Blonde2,1602,3252,5553,150

    Prices are for a Stylist, in AED, with VAT. Your price depends on your plan, your hair length and thickness, and your stylist level. You see a guide price when you book, and your salon confirms it after checking your hair. Your stylist level and your length are confirmed on WhatsApp before you book.

    Your price is held: it is the same for all three visits.

    Blonde Menu services are priced the same way, by length, in AED for a Stylist. Colour Correction is quoted for you after a full consultation. Ammonia-free colour and bleach add AED 100.

  3. What the plan is.

    Three visits, Create, Protect and Keep on Track, booked and paid together as one plan.

    Blow-dry is not included. You can add one at the regular price.

    You see the same colourist at every visit.

  4. When the visits happen.
    VisitWhenWhat happens
    1 · CreateYour first appointmentPartial highlights with Soft Blonde, half-head highlights with Signature Blonde, full-head highlights with Transformation Blonde, each with bond protection.
    2 · ProtectWeek 4 to 6 after Visit 1Toner refresh and hydration.
    3 · Keep on TrackWeek 8 to 12 after Visit 1Toner, gloss and bond care. Your next cycle is booked.

    Your next visits are booked before you leave, and no deposit is needed for them.

  5. Paying.

    The three visits are booked and paid together as one plan.

    Your salon replies on WhatsApp during salon hours, 9am to 8pm, with a time and your price.

  6. Moving an appointment.

    We ask for 48 hours’ notice to move or cancel a visit. Short notice follows our booking and deposit policy.

  7. Deposits.

    Some appointments need a deposit to hold your time, such as a longer or higher-value service, or any appointment of more than two hours. We tell you before anything is confirmed.

    A deposit is never added on top: it comes off your price on the day. Booking your next visit before you leave the salon needs no deposit, and a same-day booking never does.

    You can move an appointment up to two times with at least 48 hours’ notice, and your deposit moves with you. With less notice it is not refundable. The full details are in our booking and deposit policy.

  8. The Tara Rose Confidence Promise.

    It applies to every colour and treatment service.

    If something does not feel right within seven days of any colour or treatment service, we invite you back to refine it. No blame, just care.

    Refine, never refund.

  9. Your details.

    Tara Rose Salon is the data controller. We use what you send only to arrange your appointment, and it lands with our team, never a personal inbox. You can access, correct or delete your details any time at privacy@tararosesalon.com. See our Privacy Policy.
