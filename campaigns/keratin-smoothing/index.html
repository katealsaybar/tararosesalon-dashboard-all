Questions

Keratin & Smoothing, answered.

What is Keratin & Smoothing?
It is a salon service that coats each strand and seals the outer layer of your hair with heat. Humid air has less to grab onto, so frizz stays down, your hair dries faster and it stays smooth for months, not hours.
How long does a keratin treatment last?
Up to around six months. It softens gradually as you wash, and a sulphate-free shampoo helps it last. Many clients add a Keratin Regrowth Touch-Up at three to four months, which smooths the new growth so they never start again from zero.
How long does a keratin smoothing appointment take?
Allow two to four hours. Longer and thicker hair takes longer, and your salon confirms the time for your hair when you book.
Keratin Frizz-Free or Keratin Supreme, which one is right for me?
Choose Frizz-Free if you want to keep body and movement, and Supreme Straightening if you want your hair sleek and straight. If you are not sure, your stylist looks at your hair on your WhatsApp consultation and tells you which one suits it.
How much does keratin smoothing cost in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?
At Tara Rose it starts at AED 1,085 for short hair, AED 1,285 for medium, AED 1,400 for long and AED 1,600 for extra long. Thick hair can move you up a length, and your final price is set when your stylist sees your hair.
Can I colour my hair and have keratin smoothing?
Yes. Colour usually comes first, so the smoothing seals it in. Tell us about your colour plans on your consultation, and your stylist sets the order and timing with you.
How do I look after my hair after smoothing?
Your stylist tells you how long to wait before your first wash and before tying your hair up. After that, wash with a sulphate-free shampoo and conditioner matched to your hair. Home care is not retail. It is the insurance on your hair. It protects the result you have already paid for.
Can I have keratin smoothing while pregnant?
Please check with your doctor first, and tell us on your consultation. We will talk you through the service and the timing so you can decide what feels right.
Who does my smoothing?
One of our junior stylists or blow-dry specialists who list keratin and smoothing among their specialisms, at every salon. Each one is trained the Tara Rose way and supervised by senior team members on everything she offers you.
Do I pay a deposit, and what if something does not feel right?
Longer services like smoothing may need a deposit. It comes off your price on the day, moves with you if you give 48 hours’ notice, and we tell you before anything is confirmed. Every treatment service is covered by the Confidence Promise, set out just above where you start your consultation.
Which salons offer Keratin & Smoothing?
All four: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.
