The Hair Extension Plan

Terms & Conditions.

These terms apply to The Hair Extension Plan, available all year at Tara Rose salons in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

All prices in AED
← Back to The Hair Extension Plan
  1. Where it applies.

    The Hair Extension Plan is available all year at all four Tara Rose salons: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai. Your credit works at any of them.

    The prices and validity are those shown on the day you reserve.

  2. How prices work.

    All prices are in UAE Dirhams and include applicable value added tax.

  3. What your credit covers.

    Your credit covers haircuts, blow-dries, toning and treatments for your extensions. It covers extension-related services only. Essentials and Plus do not cover new hair. The Bundle includes your full application.

    Every plan includes extra credit on top of what you pay. AED 1,500 on Maintain Essentials gives you AED 1,800 to spend, which is AED 300 extra credit. AED 3,000 on Maintain Plus gives you AED 3,600 to spend, which is AED 600 extra credit.

    Extra credit is credit, not cash. It covers the same services as your plan and has the same validity.

  4. The New Hair and Maintain Bundle.

    The Bundle is AED 3,300. It includes your full application in your chosen method, at any Tara Rose salon, with the method and the amount of hair agreed at your Confidence Mapping.

    It gives you AED 4,250 to spend, which includes AED 950 extra credit for care, covering the same services as the other plans. The price for Keratin and Fusion is quoted at your Confidence Mapping.

  5. Move-ups.

    Move-up appointments, which move your extensions back up as your hair grows, are not part of any plan. They are booked and paid separately, and your credit, including the Bundle’s AED 950, does not cover them.

    Move-ups and tightening appointments for Tape-In, Nano Rings, Beaded Weft, Sewn Weft and Keratin and Fusion are priced at the move-up prices on the plan page. Your stylist confirms the packs, rows or hours at your Confidence Mapping.

  6. Paying.

    Reserve first, pay second. Your salon replies on WhatsApp during salon hours, 9am to 8pm, with your payment link, and your credit goes on your account once payment clears.

    You can split Maintain Plus into 4 payments of AED 750, and the Bundle into 4 payments of AED 825, with Tabby. Your credit stays the same. Maintain Essentials is paid by cash or card. Tabby’s own terms apply to a split payment.

  7. How long it lasts.
    PlanValid
    Maintain Essentials6 months from the day your payment is confirmed
    Maintain Plus9 months from the day your payment is confirmed
    New Hair and Maintain Bundle12 months from the day your payment is confirmed

    Validity runs from the day your payment is confirmed, including when you pay with Tabby. Unused credit expires at the end of the validity period.

  8. December and public holidays.

    Your credit, including your extra credit, cannot be used in December or on a public holiday.

    The days of December and the public holidays that fall inside your validity do not count against it. Your validity is extended by those days, so you do not lose any credit to them.

  9. Priority last-minute slots.

    On Maintain Plus and the Bundle, when a same-day or next-day space opens at your salon, our team offers it to you first. This depends on availability, so a slot is not always open. Priority slots are not offered in December or on public holidays.

  10. Refunds.

    Plans are not refundable.

    If something unexpected happens, message us and our management team will review your case with care.

  11. Gifts.

    A plan bought as a gift is issued in her name, ready to book, with a note from you. It cannot be moved to someone else after that. Its validity starts from the day the payment is confirmed, like any other plan.

  12. Moving an appointment.

    We ask for 48 hours’ notice to move or cancel an appointment. Short notice follows our booking and deposit policy.

  13. The Tara Rose Confidence Promise.

    It applies to every colour and treatment service.

    If something does not feel right within seven days of any colour or treatment service, we invite you back to refine it. No blame, just care.

    Refine, never refund.

  14. Your details.

    Tara Rose Salon is the data controller. We use what you send only to manage your plan. You can access, correct or delete your details any time at privacy@tararosesalon.com. See our Privacy Policy.
