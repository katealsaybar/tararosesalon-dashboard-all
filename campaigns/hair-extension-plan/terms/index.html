The Hair Extension Plan

Terms & Conditions.

These terms apply to The Hair Extension Plan, available all year at Tara Rose salons in Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

All prices in AED
← Back to The Hair Extension Plan
  1. Where it applies.

    Credit is valid across all Tara Rose salons in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

    The Hair Extension Plan is available all year, and the prices and validity shown are those on the day you reserve.

  2. How prices work.

    All figures are in UAE Dirhams and include applicable value added tax.

  3. What the plans cover.

    Maintain Essentials and Maintain Plus cover cuts, blow-dries, toning and gloss, treatments, scalp scrubs and head and neck massage, and do not cover new hair.

    The Maintenance Bonus (AED 300 on Maintain Essentials, AED 600 on Maintain Plus) is added to your plan as credit, not cash, and has the same validity as the plan. The New Hair and Maintain Bundle has no Maintenance Bonus.

  4. The New Hair and Maintain Bundle.

    The New Hair and Maintain Bundle includes your full application, in your chosen method, at any Tara Rose salon, with the method and the amount of hair agreed at your Confidence Mapping.

    Its price is the application price for your method plus the AED 1,500 credit.

  5. Move-ups.

    Move-up appointments are not part of any plan and are booked and paid separately. Neither a plan nor the Bundle’s AED 1,500 credit covers a move-up appointment.

    Move-up and tightening appointments for Tape-In, Nano Rings, Beaded Weft, Sewn Weft and Keratin and Fusion are booked and paid separately, at the move-up prices shown, and your stylist confirms the packs, rows or hours at your Confidence Mapping.

  6. How long they last.
    PlanValid
    Maintain Essentials6 months from the day your payment is confirmed
    Maintain Plus9 months from the day your payment is confirmed
    New Hair and Maintain Bundle12 months from the day your payment is confirmed

    Validity runs from the day your payment is confirmed, including when you pay with Tabby. Unused credit expires at the end of the validity period.

  7. Priority last-minute slots.

    Priority last-minute slots on Maintain Plus and the Bundle mean that when a same-day or next-day space opens at your salon, our team offers it to you first. It depends on availability, the time is not guaranteed, and priority slots are not offered in December or on public holidays.

  8. Paying with Tabby.

    Tabby’s own terms apply to a split payment.

  9. Refunds.

    Plans are not refundable.

    If something unexpected happens, message us and our management team will review your case with care.

  10. Gifts.

    A plan bought as a gift is issued in her name at purchase and cannot be moved again after that.

  11. Moving an appointment.

    We ask for 48 hours’ notice to move an appointment, and short notice follows our booking and deposit policy.
