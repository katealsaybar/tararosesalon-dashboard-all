Why a plan

Why extensions need looking after.

Here’s what’s actually happening

Your own hair keeps growing underneath, so every week the point where your extensions attach sits a little further from your scalp. That is what you see as the blend starting to show.

What this means for you

We move the attachment back up, check the tension and look after the blend, on a rhythm set by your method and how fast your hair grows.

Questions

Quick answers.

Does the plan cover new hair?
Only the Bundle. It includes your full application plus AED 950 to spend on care, valid 12 months after purchase. Essentials and Plus are for extensions you already wear.
Does the credit cover my move-up?
No. Move-ups are booked and paid as they are today. Your credit covers the care around them that keeps the blend looking new.
Can I split the payment?
Yes, with Tabby. Plus splits into 4 payments of AED 750, and the Bundle into 4 payments of AED 825. Your credit stays the same. Essentials is cash or card.
Can I give it as a gift?
Yes. The plan is issued in her name, ready to book, with a note from you.
