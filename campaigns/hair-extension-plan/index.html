Why a plan

Why extensions need looking after.

01

Here’s what’s actually happening

Your own hair keeps growing underneath, so every week the point where your extensions attach sits a little further from your scalp. That is what you see as the blend starting to show.

02

What this means for you

We move the attachment back up, check the tension and look after the blend, on a rhythm set by your method and how fast your hair grows.

Questions

Quick answers.

Does the plan cover new hair?
Only the New Hair and Maintain Bundle does. It includes your full application in your chosen method, plus a 12-month Maintenance Bundle credit of AED 1,500. Maintain Essentials and Maintain Plus are for extensions you already wear.
Does the credit cover my move-up appointment?
No. Your move-up is booked and paid as it is today. The plan covers everything around it that keeps the blend looking new.
Can I split the payment?
Yes. Maintain Plus splits into 4 interest-free payments of AED 750, and the New Hair and Maintain Bundle into 4 payments, from AED 825 each, with Tabby. The credit stays exactly the same, and so does the Maintenance Bonus on Maintain Essentials and Maintain Plus. Maintain Essentials is cash or card only.
Can I give it as a gift?
Yes. The plan is issued in her name, ready to book, with a note from you.
