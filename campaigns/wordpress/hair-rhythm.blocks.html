The rhythm

Colour day is week one. Here is what comes next.

Most of us leave colour until it has faded, then start again. A short visit in between keeps it looking right all the way to your next colour day.

The Hair Rhythm

  1. Colour dayWeek 1Bright, even, finished.
  2. The Tone ResetWeek 4 to 5Toner refreshed before the warmth comes back.
  3. A treatment or a blow-dryWeek 8Your stylist picks what your hair needs to get its shine back.
  4. Colour againWeek 10It still looks right, so there is less to fix on colour day.

Beautiful hair starts with understanding your hair.

The best maintenance routine isn’t the same for everyone. Through our 8-Step Hair Plan, your stylist considers your hair’s condition, colour, needs and goals before recommending the right services. It’s a more considered approach to maintaining your hair, rather than simply choosing a treatment from a menu. Your routine can evolve as your hair changes.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is a junior stylist experienced enough?​
She is a specialist in the appointments she does most. Every one of them is trained the Tara Rose way and supervised by senior team members on everything she offers you.
How often should I come in?​
Your stylist will talk you through a rhythm that suits your hair. A common one is colour, a Tone Reset at week four or five, a blow-dry or treatment around week eight, then colour again around week ten.
What does it cost?​
Tone Resets start at AED 220, treatments at AED 210 and blow-dry packages from AED 264. Your price depends on the service and your length, and your salon confirms the final price.
How long do packages last?​
The Blow-dry Edit and Series are valid for one month from purchase, Four Weeks for six weeks from your first booking and treatment courses for three months (a course of three) or six months (a course of five). Moving an appointment needs 48 hours’ notice, and prepaid sessions are not refundable. Prepaid sessions cannot be booked in December or on public holidays, and your validity is extended by those days.
How do deposits and the Confidence Promise work?​
Some appointments need a deposit, such as longer or higher-value services. It comes off your price on the day, moves with you if you give 48 hours’ notice, and we tell you before anything is confirmed: our deposit policy. Every colour and treatment service is covered by the Confidence Promise, set out just above where you book.
Which salons?​
All four: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.
