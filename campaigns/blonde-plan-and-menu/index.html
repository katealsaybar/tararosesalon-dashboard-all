Questions

Quick answers.

How do I know which lane I am?
Soft Blonde is brightness with lower upkeep. Signature Blonde is for women who identify as blonde and want a regular rhythm. Transformation Blonde is for a major change, high lift or correction. You do not have to be right: the Confidence Mapping names it with you. Balayage sits inside whichever lane you are in.
How often will I need to come in?
Visit 2 sits at week four to six, and visit 3 at week eight to twelve. Your stylist names your exact weeks in the chair, and you leave with the next two visits booked.
Will lightening damage my hair?
Healthy blonde is possible when protection is built in from the start. Bond protection runs through every blonde service here, before, during and after your colour.
What does it cost?
The Confidence Mapping is free. It maps what your blonde needs and what it will cost, before you commit to anything.
