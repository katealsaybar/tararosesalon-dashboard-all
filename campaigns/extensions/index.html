Extensions, explained

Why extensions need maintenance.

  1. What you might have been told

    They go in once and stay put until you’re ready to take them out.

  2. Here’s what’s actually happening

    Your own hair grows about 1 to 1.5cm a month, so the point of attachment moves away from your scalp and the blend starts to show.

  3. What this means for you

    A move-up timed to your method brings them back to the root and keeps your own hair safe underneath. The rhythm is what makes the extensions last.

How it works

Fitting day is week one. Here is what comes next.

Your own hair keeps growing underneath, roughly 1 to 1.5cm a month, and it carries your extensions away from the root. A move-up, timed to your method, brings them back before the blend shows.

Your extensions rhythm

  1. Fitting dayWeek 1Seamless, full and matched to your colour and length.
  2. Between visitsWeek 2 onwardsA cut, treatment or scalp check keeps the lengths soft and the blend clean.
  3. Your move-upWeek 6 to 12A removal and refit, timed to your method, before anything slips or tangles.
  4. Back on rhythmEvery visitYour scalp and hairline are checked, so your own hair stays healthy underneath.

An example rhythm, not a fixed schedule. Your stylist sets the timing for your method and how quickly your hair grows.

Questions

Extensions, answered.

Which extension method is right for my hair?
It depends on your hair type and what you want them to do. As a guide, Tape-In and Nano Rings are often chosen for fine hair, Beaded Weft for medium to thick hair, Sewn Weft for thick hair and the most volume, and Keratin & Fusion for the most natural movement. Your stylist matches the method, and the weight of hair, to what your own hair can carry comfortably at your free Confidence Mapping, so the right method starts with understanding your hair.
What is the difference between tapes, wefts, rings and bonds?
Tape-In panels sit flat against the root. Nano Rings hold small sections with a tiny ring, with no glue or heat. Beaded and Sewn Weft attach a full row of hair, held at beads or sewn onto a fine braid, for the most volume. Keratin & Fusion bonds attach strand by strand and move most like your own hair.
Why do extensions need maintenance?
Your own hair keeps growing underneath, roughly 1 to 1.5cm a month, so every week the point of attachment sits a little further from your scalp and the blend starts to show. Most women are sold the fitting and never told about the rhythm that keeps it. That was never yours to work out alone. A move-up is a removal and refit that brings them back up to the root. The rhythm is what makes the extensions last.
How often do I need a move-up?
It is set at your consultation and depends on your method and how quickly your hair grows. As a guide, Tape-In and Sewn Weft every 6 to 8 weeks, Nano Rings and Beaded Weft every 8 to 10, and Keratin & Fusion every 10 to 12.
How much do hair extensions cost in Dubai and Abu Dhabi?
New hair is priced by method and the amount of hair you need, at your free Confidence Mapping. New hair starts from AED 1,800 for Weft, AED 2,200 for Tape-In and AED 2,800 for Nano Rings. The New Hair & Maintain Bundle is AED 6,000, which works out at around AED 500 a month for your full application plus 12 months of care. Move-ups start at AED 450 a pack for Tape-In and AED 450 a row for Sewn and Beaded Weft. Nano Rings and Keratin & Fusion are AED 500 an hour.
Do you charge to remove extensions fitted somewhere else?
Taking out Tape-In, Nano Rings or weft extensions is included when you are having new hair fitted with us. Keratin & Fusion bonds take longer to remove, so removal is charged by the hour.
Do hair extensions damage your natural hair?
Not when they are fitted by a trained stylist and kept to the move-up rhythm for your method. Damage comes from missed move-ups, the wrong method for your hair or too much tension. Tension left too long can cause traction alopecia, hair loss from ongoing pulling. Caught early it is reversible, which is why we check your scalp and hairline at every visit.
How do I look after my extensions?
Wash with extension-safe, sulphate-free home care and put moisture back through the mid-lengths and ends, as extension hair has no scalp feeding it. Plait or tie your hair loosely before bed and keep your styling loose, as tight ponytails and buns pull on the point of attachment.
Can I spread the cost, or give it as a gift?
Yes. Maintain Plus splits into 4 interest-free payments of AED 750 with Tabby, and the New Hair & Maintain Bundle into 4 of AED 1,500. Maintain Essentials is cash or card. Any package can be issued in her name, ready to book, with a note from you.
Do I pay a deposit, and what if something does not feel right?
A refundable AED 500 deposit secures every fitting and comes off your final bill. Cancellations need 48 hours’ notice. Every treatment service is covered by the Confidence Promise, set out just above where you start your consultation.
