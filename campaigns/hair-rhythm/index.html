The rhythm

Colour day is week one. Here is what comes next.

Most of us leave colour until it has faded, then start again. A short visit in between keeps it looking right all the way to your next colour day.

Without a rhythm

  1. Colour dayWeek 1Bright, even, finished.
  2. Warmth showsWeek 4 to 5The toner has worn off.
  3. Dull and dryWeek 8The shine has gone and the roots are showing.
  4. The big fixWeek 10More time in the chair, more stress on your hair, more cost.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is a junior stylist experienced enough?
She is a specialist in the appointments she does most. Every one of them is trained the Tara Rose way and supervised by senior team members on everything she offers you.
How often should I come in?
Your stylist will talk you through a rhythm that suits your hair. A common one is colour, a Tone Reset at week four or five, a blow-dry or treatment around week eight, then colour again around week ten.
What does it cost?
Tone Resets start at AED 200, treatments at AED 260 and blow-dry packages from AED 264. Your price depends on the service and your length, and your salon confirms it on WhatsApp before you book.
How long do packages last?
The Blow-dry Edit and Series are valid for three months from purchase, Four Weeks for six weeks from your first booking and treatment courses for three months (a course of three) or six months (a course of five). Moving an appointment needs 48 hours’ notice, and prepaid sessions are not refundable.
How do deposits and the Confidence Promise work?
Some appointments need a deposit, such as longer or higher-value services. It comes off your price on the day, moves with you if you give 48 hours’ notice, and we tell you before anything is confirmed: our deposit policy. Every colour and treatment service is covered by the Confidence Promise, set out just above where you book.
Which salons?
All four: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.

What would you like?

Highlighted or foiled roots take longer and sit with The Blonde Standard, not The Hair Rhythm. Send us a message and we will match you with the right stylist.

Would you like more than one treatment?

Optional. Add up to four more, for five treatments in all in one session.

Would you like a single visit or a course?

A course is paid once, with the same stylist tracking your result: three sessions valid 3 months, or five sessions valid 6 months.

Where does your hair fall?

Check from the back, with your hair dry and brushed straight. Our short may be shorter than you think.

How much hair do you have?

Tie a ponytail with a standard hair tie and count the wraps. Thick hair takes more time and product, so it can move you up a length.
