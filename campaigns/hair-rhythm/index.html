Questions

Quick answers.

Is a junior stylist experienced enough?
She is a specialist in the appointments she does most. Every one of them is trained the Tara Rose way and supervised by senior team members on everything she offers you.
How often should I come in?
Your stylist writes your rhythm down for you. A common one is colour, a Tone Reset at week four or five, a blow-dry or treatment around week eight, then colour again around week ten.
Which salons?
All four: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.
What is a Tone Reset?
Toner and a blow-dry, thirty to forty-five minutes. It resets your tone before the warmth creeps back in, which is why week eight can still look like week one.
