Terms & Conditions.

These terms apply to The Hair Rhythm, available at all four Tara Rose salons: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.

All prices in AED

  1. Where it applies.

    The Hair Rhythm is available at all four Tara Rose salons: Khalifa City A and Mamsha Al Saadiyat in Abu Dhabi, Al Quoz 1 and Motor City in Dubai.

  2. How prices work.

    Every price shown is a starting price for short or fine hair, in AED, and is the price on the day you book.

    Your salon confirms your price for your length and thickness on WhatsApp before anything is booked, never at the till. Colour refreshes and Keratin smoothing are priced the same way, by your hair length, and confirmed before you book.

    Keratin starts with a WhatsApp consultation, and we confirm the full price there.

  3. Prepaid packages.

    The Blow-dry Edit, the Blow-dry Series, Four Weeks of Blow-dries and Treatment Courses are paid once and booked with your salon as you need them, at any of the four salons. We do our best to keep you with the same stylist.

    Four Weeks of Blow-dries is two blow-dries a week across four weeks, with the slots held in advance.

  4. Keratin and your root touch-up.

    Make it a year adds a root touch-up visit to your keratin, for AED 500 to 600 on top of your keratin price. We ask you to book the visit within 3 to 4 months. If you come back after 6 months, you may need a full keratin service again, which uses more product and costs extra. Your salon tells you the price before anything is booked.

  5. Who it is for and what is not included.

    The Hair Rhythm prices apply to our junior stylists and stylists. Senior stylists and style directors are not part of The Hair Rhythm, and their services are priced separately.

    Colour maintenance is not available for bleach, scalp bleach, platinum, maximum brightness or anything that needs bleaching. If your hair is bleached, please book a consultation first. Colour correction is quoted for you, and a toner after a root colour is booked on its own, at its regular price.

  7. Refunds and missed appointments.

    Prepaid sessions are not refundable.

    An appointment missed without 48 hours’ notice counts as a used session.

  8. Moving an appointment.

    We ask for 48 hours’ notice to move or cancel an appointment. Short notice follows our booking and deposit policy.

  9. Deposits.

    Some appointments need a deposit to hold your time, such as a longer or higher-value service, or any appointment of more than two hours. We tell you before anything is confirmed.

    A deposit is never added on top: it comes off your price on the day. Booking your next visit before you leave the salon needs no deposit, and a same-day booking never does.

    You can move an appointment up to two times with at least 48 hours’ notice, and your deposit moves with you. With less notice it is not refundable. The full details are in our booking and deposit policy.

  10. The Tara Rose Confidence Promise.

    It applies to every colour and treatment service.

    If something does not feel right within seven days of any colour or treatment service, we invite you back to refine it. No blame, just care.

    Refine, never refund.

  11. Your details.

    Tara Rose Salon is the data controller. We use what you send only to arrange your appointment, and it lands with our team, never a personal inbox. You can access, correct or delete your details any time at privacy@tararosesalon.com. See our Privacy Policy.
