The full terms, and what your credit covers
All figures are in UAE Dirhams and include applicable value-added tax. Your credit is a balance in Dirhams, and it spends at our menu price on the day you use it. If our prices change during your validity, your credit is worth what it is worth on the day, the same as money. Your credit spends on any service at any of our UAE salons, on the services that salon offers; where a service is not offered at your salon, it is booked for you at the nearest salon that offers it. It also spends on retail, including home care, and on new hair extensions, at any of our UAE salons. Where a bill comes to more than your balance, you pay the difference in the usual way. On every tier, the Self-Care Bonus added on top is for beauty, treatments, keratin and blow-dries, and neither the bonus nor your credit can be used in December or on any public holiday, Eid included. Your Self-Care Bonus is redeemed with a junior stylist, a blow-dry specialist or a beautician. Your voucher cannot be used alongside any other offer. Holidays. Our salons close between Christmas and New Year, and the closure dates are published at each salon and on our website before December. Any voucher bought or gifted over that period is issued as normal and booked from the day we reopen, and the days we are closed do not count against your validity. Your credit and your Self-Care Bonus cannot be used in December or on any public holiday, Eid included. Where a public holiday falls inside your validity and we are closed, your validity is extended by the days we were shut. Payment is taken by a secure link we send you, handled by Stripe; your card details go to them and are not held by us or by the salon. Your credit is on your account once the payment clears. Dip Your Toes is paid in full. Season of You and All-In VIP Year can be split into four with Tabby, and the credit stays exactly the same. What it is worth is your credit and Self-Care Bonus, plus the gift voucher and the birthday service at menu price. Your voucher is for one person. It is held in your name, it is not transferable, it cannot be shared, split or passed on, and it is not refundable or exchangeable for money, with one exception. Questions

The honest answers.

Is this money off?
No. It is your own plan, paid forward. You set money aside and we add more credit on top, so every visit you were always going to make is worth more.
How does the credit work?
Your credit spends on any service, at every tier: colour, cuts, beauty and treatments. On every tier, the Self-Care Bonus added on top is for beauty, treatments, keratin and blow-dries, and neither the bonus nor your credit can be used in December or on any public holiday, Eid included. Your credit also spends on retail, including home care, and on new hair extensions. Your bonus is redeemed with a junior stylist, a blow-dry specialist or a beautician, and it does not cover home care or new hair extensions. Your voucher cannot be used alongside any other offer. Once it is on your account it stays yours until it is used or your validity date passes, and none of it is taken back before then.
What if your prices go up before I use it?
Your credit is a balance in Dirhams, so it behaves like money: it spends at our menu price on the day you use it. If our prices move while your voucher is running, your credit is worth what it is worth on the day. The same is true of your Self-Care Bonus and your gift voucher, because those are amounts too. Your birthday service is not an amount; it is a service, so you have it whatever the menu says when you take it.
What if I have to leave the UAE?
Tell us. If you are leaving unexpectedly, we refund the credit you have not used, pro rata. It is the one exception to the rule that a voucher is not exchangeable for money, and it exists because this is a city people leave at short notice, and because holding your money while you are on the other side of the world is not looking after you.
What if I do not use it all?
Your credit is held for the whole of your window: six months, nine or twelve, depending on the voucher you choose. That is longer than the rhythm most women keep, so it is built to be used without rushing, and we put the visits in the diary with you at your Confidence Mapping. What is not used by your validity date is not carried over.
Is there any small print at the till?
No surprises. Your credit covers the full menu price of any service, and your quote is agreed before, never at the till. The credit moves with you between our salons, because the plan is the same at every one. Not every salon offers every service: Motor City is hair only, and Al Quoz is hair and beauty. If yours does not offer what you want, we book it for you at the nearest salon that does, and your credit goes with you.
How do I pay?
By a secure payment link we send you on WhatsApp. It is handled by Stripe, so your card details go straight to them and are never held by us or by the salon. Dip Your Toes is paid in full. Season of You and All-In VIP Year can be split into four with Tabby at no extra cost, which is a separate agreement with Tabby under their terms.
Can I split the payment?
Yes. Season of You splits into four payments of AED 625, and All-In VIP Year into four of AED 1,125, with Tabby, at no extra cost to you. The credit, the bonus and the gifts stay exactly the same. Dip Your Toes is paid in full.
What happens after I reserve?
We message you on WhatsApp to confirm your tier and send you a secure payment link. It is handled by Stripe, so your card details go to them and never to us. Your credit is on your account as soon as it is paid. Then we book your Confidence Mapping, a free read of your hair before your first visit, so the plan is written down with you rather than decided in the chair. If you would rather talk it through first, reply to that message and we will call.
Can I give it as a gift?
Yes. The voucher is sent in her name, ready to book, with a note from you. A gift of being looked after, not another thing she did not ask for. Our salons close between Christmas and New Year, so a voucher given over Christmas is booked from the New Year.
Can I share it, or pass it on?
Your credit is yours alone. It is held in your name. It cannot be shared, split or passed to anyone else, and it cannot be exchanged for money. The gift voucher inside your tier is the part you give away, and when the woman you give it to visits, your credit grows. If you are buying the whole voucher for someone else, we issue it in her name from the start, so it is hers.
Can I bring more than one friend?
Yes. Your gift voucher is the first way in. Ask us for more, AED 50 each, for as many friends as you like while the window is open. Every friend who visits adds AED 50 to your credit. When three have visited, a facial or a scalp and hair treatment with a blow-dry is on us, at any of our salons. When five have, you are invited to the next window before it opens, with first choice of the 45.
What if I miss this window?
These forty-five are the last of this year, so the next window is next year. Leave us your details and we will tell you the day it opens, before it goes anywhere else.

Your credit and your bonus spend at any of the four, on whatever that salon offers. If your salon does not do the service you want, we book it for you at the nearest one that does, and your credit goes with you.
