What net take means. Everything the salon billed in the period: hair and beauty services, treatments, and retail, added together, before staff cost. It is the same figure the dashboard's receipt calls net take; the old "money in" wording is gone.

Sources. Benchmarks, client funnel and the headline three come from the live dashboard's weekly view (1 Jul – 12 Aug 2026). Wins and Branch Performance are queried from phorest_staff_daily. Both feeds run to 12 Aug, so the AED 111,170 between the branch total (AED 2.06m) and the weekly net take (AED 1.95m) is not a reporting lag: the two are counting different things. Unreconciled, and flagged here rather than hidden. Anything marked SAMPLE is a placeholder target.

The left spine. The dashboard's own app nav, same ink panel and same items. This file is the KPI Metrics view and holds no others, so the pill moves but nothing loads. The mini index inside the page is live and does scroll you.

Filters. Branch and period are the real controls, wired to the same two choices the live dashboard offers. This page holds one fixed pull, so changing them re-labels the view and re-focuses Branch Performance; every other figure stays at All Branches, 1 Jul to 12 Aug. The live build re-queries here.

Layout rules. One token set (the dashboard's), 10px radius, 8pt spacing spine, Playfair for figures and Inter for labels. Colour carries status only; the accent quartet carries identity. Three headline cards, never twenty-one. Anything below target sorts by the size of its gap. No data, no card.
